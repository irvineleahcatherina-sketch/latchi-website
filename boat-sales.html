Signature day

Your own boat to the Blue Lagoon.

No crowd on deck, no timetable. Follow the Akamas coast west from Latchi, stop wherever the water looks best, and be back for a late lunch at the harbour.

  1. 09:00
    Latchi harbourFull briefing, cool box on board, then west along the coast. Keep to 5 kph in the harbour.
  2. 09:20
    Aphrodite's Rock & St George's IslandFirst swim. Look out for the wild seabirds that nest on the island.
  3. 10:00
    Amphitheatre & Manolis BayAn old Roman stone quarry, with carved limestone walls and sea caves you can swim into.
  4. 10:45
    The Blue LagoonTwo sides, a small one and a big one, with a reef between them. Anchor in either. To switch, drive out to open water and back in. Never cross the reef.
  5. 11:45
    Fontana Amorosa & the beachTurn back at the reef before the cape. Past it there's no radio signal.
  6. 13:00
    Back to LatchiFish tavernas line the harbour wall.

Safety first, then fun

Never driven a boat? You'll be fine.

Our safety boats patrol the Akamas every day, crewed by the most highly trained team in Latchi. We're an RYA Training Centre and a PADI 5-Star Instructor Development Resort, so we train instructors, not just guests. Everyone gets a full briefing and basic training before leaving, and every boat has a VHF radio and GPS tracker.

A dedicated RIB and crew watching the coastline every day you're out.

Questions we get every day

Do I need a boat licence?

No. In Cyprus, visitors can drive our boats with a valid car driving licence. The driver needs to be 21 or over, and everyone gets a full briefing and basic training before leaving.

I've never driven a boat. Is that OK?

Most of our guests haven't. The briefing covers everything, our team can take you for a short practice run, and our safety boat patrols the coast all day.

What happens if there's swell?

When the sea is up, we send the boats out with one of our captains at the helm, so you still get your day on the water. For changes and refunds, see our refunds and cancellations policy.

How far is the Blue Lagoon?

About 6 nautical miles west of Latchi along the Akamas coast. Allow around 35 minutes each way in a smaller boat and longer if you stop at the coves.

Can we bring food and drink?

Yes. Every boat has a cool box, so bring lunch, drinks and ice. Please take all your rubbish back with you. The Akamas is a nature reserve.

How long can I hire a boat for?

Most guests take 4 hours, either 09:00 to 13:00 or 14:00 to 18:00. That's plenty for the Blue Lagoon and a couple of coves. You can also book 1 or 2 hours, or a full day of 6 hours.

Is fuel included?

No. You leave with a full tank and we refuel when you're back, so you only pay for what you used, at the market rate. Just like car hire.

Charter questions

Do we need any boating experience?

None at all. This is your holiday, so lie back and relax. Your captain and crew do everything for you: they drive, find the best swimming spots, anchor, and serve the fruit and drinks. All you need to do is enjoy the day.

Is there a deposit?

No. Charters are paid in full when you book.

What happens if there's swell?

Your captain chooses the most sheltered route for the day. For changes and refunds, see our refunds and cancellations policy.

Can we add a Seabob or catering?

Yes, and much more. We'd love to make your day exactly how you want it. Just tell us about any special wishes, whether that's kosher, vegetarian or any other food, your favourite drinks, a birthday cake or decorations for a wedding or proposal, and we'll arrange everything.
